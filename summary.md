Project Summary
I first designed a circuit in easyeda using esp32, digital to analog converter, op-amps, switches, LDO's, etc for the purpose of making my own DIY amperometric device, which can perform chronoamperomtery studies. I then routed all the connections, star delta configuration etc to separate analog gnd from digital gnd, routed all the traces and designed the physical PCB. I then ordered the PCB from JLCPCB. Then, I uploaded the code/firmware to the esp32 using an FTDI programmer chip after many iterations, developed a one-click web based app interface for the device wherein we can change lots of parameters including the applied potential, duration, interval, range, etc, as well as a large window for vieweing the results/graphs, as well as export capabiilities in the form of .csv files or JSON files or ordinary copy as text. I then designed a case for the device in Fusion 360, and printed it out in pla using my prusa mini printer. Now, for the chenical validation and characterizatioon steps: First, I took different concentrations of Zobel solution-ferri/ferrocyanide [Fe(CN)₆]³⁻/⁴⁻ redox couple, and tested them out ( We dilted it using Pbs buffer) Then, we did the same with RuHex and tested the different dilutions. Then, we started to test our setup with  already established fabricated electrodes and analyte pairs like Hydrogen peroXide and gold. For the second analyte, we took p-cresol and its different dilutions/concentrations, ranging from 1femto molar, pico molar, nano molar, mili molar etc. For the third analyte, we are still thinking, probably we will just report these 4 use cases, out of which 2 are direct electron transfer molecules. Thus, our intent is to make an end to end portable electrochemical detection device with ultra low sensitivity at a fraction of the cost (PalmSense potentiostat cost arund 5 lakhs to procure in my lab, these too only 5k-7k rupees)
